# Socialville Welcome (05)

Build a warm, welcoming website for Socialville Baptist Church, a small Baptist church in Mason, Ohio that has served the community since 1962. The site should feel traditional, trustworthy, and inviting — not flashy or corporate. Think warm neutrals, a serif or classic sans-serif font, and lots of white space. Avoid anything that feels overly modern/startup-like; this is a family-oriented church community site.

Pages / Sections

1. Home

Hero section with church name, the verse "This is the Lord's doing; it is marvellous in our eyes." — Psalm 118:23, and a welcoming line: "Faithfully serving the Mason area since 1962."
Short intro inviting new visitors, those just passing through, or anyone looking for a church home to visit.
Service times displayed clearly (see below).
A "Plan Your Visit" or "We'd Love to See You" call-to-action button linking to the Visit/Contact section.

2. About / Our History

Summary of the church's founding in 1962 and growth over the decades (organized May 9, 1962; incorporated June 9, 1962; original building purchased from a former flexography plant).
Timeline-style layout (by decade: 1960s, 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, 2000s+) highlighting key milestones: first pastor Bro. Howard Carter (1962), building paid off (1974), new sanctuary groundbreaking (1981) and dedication (1982), building paid off (1988), and more recent renovations.
List of past and current pastors, ending with current Pastor Stan Sexton (serving since December 1993).
Include the verse: "Therefore my beloved brethren, be ye steadfast, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord..." — 1 Corinthians 15:58 (note: verse in source says 15:5, but the quoted text corresponds to 15:58; verify and use the correct reference).

3. Ministries

Highlight that the church currently supports 18 foreign, home, and evangelistic ministries.
Feature cards or sections for:
Great Commission Outreach — "Where no one is too far away" (Mark 16:15)
Nursing Home Ministry — "Where no one is forgotten" (Psalm 71:9)
Youth/Juvenile Detention Ministry — "Where no one is beyond help" (Matthew 25:36)
Military Prayer Support — "Where everyone is serving" (2 Corinthians 13:13)
Heavenly Harvest Food Pantry

4. Service Times

Sunday School — 10:00 AM
Sunday Worship — 11:00 AM
Sunday Evening — 6:00 PM
Wednesday — 7:00 PM
Display this prominently, ideally also in the header or footer so it's visible on every page.

5. Visit / Contact

Address: 5946 Socialville-Foster Rd, Mason, OH 45040
Phone: (513) 398-2961
Email: socialvillebaptist@gmail.com
Embedded Google Map showing the location.
Link/icon to the church's Facebook page.
Pastor's name: Pastor Stan Sexton.
Design Notes
Warm, welcoming color palette (soft creams, warm wood tones, deep blue or burgundy accents) — avoid cold corporate blues/grays.
Simple, easy navigation: Home, About/History, Ministries, Visit/Contact.
Mobile-friendly and easy to read for an older congregation as well as new visitors.
Include a footer on every page with address, phone, service times, and Facebook link.
Keep the tone warm and personal throughout — this is a small community church, not a mega-church. and no pictures
